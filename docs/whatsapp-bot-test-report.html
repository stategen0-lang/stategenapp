WhatsApp Bot Test Report

Simulated agents held 762 conversations with the new Claude-powered WhatsApp assistant, in four rounds, with fixes between rounds. Every reply was checked against the agency's real data. Every saved change was checked to confirm the right record got the right value, and only after a yes.

Model: Claude Haiku 5.5 for the bot, the simulated agents and the grader. Data: in-memory copies of Test Agency and Equity Properties. Nothing real was changed, and no WhatsApp message or email was sent.

Honest limits

Still not perfect

  • Wording on corrections. When an agent corrects a draft, Haiku sometimes writes "I've updated the budget to $1,300" before the confirmation card. The card right after it says "Reply YES to save", and nothing saves without that yes. In every one of these cases the right value was saved after the yes.
  • Small invented details, rarely. In a few long Arabic replies Haiku added a wrong detail, once placing a listing in Sidon instead of Sin el Fil. It happened in about 2 of every 100 replies in the later rounds. Prices, bedrooms and rent/sale are now checked against the data and were almost always right.
  • Marketing emails were tested up to the send. The simulator blocks all email, so the request, confirmation and yes were tested, but not delivery. Your own live test earlier showed delivery works.
  • The grader is Haiku too. It is strict and sometimes wrong, so every flag it raised was read by hand before counting anything as a bug.
  • Rate limit. Your Anthropic account allows 500,000 input tokens a minute. Only parallel testing hit it. Live traffic uses a tiny fraction.
Method

How the testing worked

  • A second Claude played a Lebanese real-estate agent with one goal, such as "raise Rita's budget to 1000" or "find 2-bed rentals in Achrafieh". It wrote in one of seven styles: clear English, terse, Arabizi, Arabic, typos, chatty, or forwarded notes.
  • Its messages went through the exact code production runs, against an in-memory copy of the agency. The simulator removes every credential except the AI providers' and blocks all other network access, so nothing could be written or sent.
  • After each conversation the copy was checked: the right record, the right value, and saved only after approval. Every reply was checked for invented listings, wrong prices, bedrooms or rent/sale, "saved" claims without a save, give-ups and privacy leaks, then graded.
  • The simulator lives in the project (scripts/agent-sim.mjs) and can be rerun after any change.
